Shimmer3R GSR+ : what is what, and where it goes

Use only the GSR+ unit (SR48-8-1). Leave the ExG unit (SR47-8-1) in the box and switched off: this page cannot read it.

1. The parts in your kit

Simplified drawings, adjusted after looking at a photo of the kit. The black coil with a round snap button is not part of this list (probably ExG): leave it out.

1GSR+ unit
White box, Shimmer3R GSR+ SR48-8-1, label BTID 90D7. Top: orange round button (power) and two small light bumps. One short end has three round sockets: black, red, black. Leave the small orange slide switch and the orange slot alone.
2Wrist strap
Holds the box on the wrist.
3GSR electrodes (x2)
One black Velcro strip with two grey round pads side by side, and two thin black leads ending in thin pins. The pads touch the palm side of the index and middle finger, the strip wraps round both fingers. Measures sweat (skin conductance).
4Optical pulse probe
White cable with a small white pod (the light sensor) and a spring clip, plus a black Velcro ring. Measures heartbeat. Your kit has two.

2. Where each part goes on the person

  1. Prepare. Hands clean and dry, no cream. Use the hand the person uses less (left if they write with the right). Arm resting on a table, palm up or relaxed.
  2. 1 + 2 Box. Put the box in the wrist strap, strap on the wrist like a watch. Snug, must not slide.
  3. 3 GSR. The two grey pads go on the palm side of the index and middle finger, on the middle segment (between the two lower knuckles). Wrap the Velcro strip around both fingers. Snug, not tight.
  4. 4 Pulse probe. Easiest and steadiest: clip the white pod on the earlobe. Or on the ring finger of the same hand: put the pod flat on the fingertip pad and wrap the black Velcro ring around it. Snug, not squeezing: if the fingertip turns white or tingles, loosen it. Keep it still.
  5. Plugs. The box has three round sockets in a row: black, red, black. Match by size: the thick earphone-style plug (pulse probe) goes in the biggest socket; the two thin pins (GSR leads) go in the two smaller sockets. Never force a plug: if it does not slide in, it is the wrong socket. The connection log checks it: "no PPG channel" means the probe is in the wrong socket; a flat skin-conductance line means the GSR pins are.
  6. Switch on the box (light on), then wait about 2 minutes before recording.

Placement (index + middle for GSR, ring finger or ear lobe for the pulse, non-dominant hand) is common practice and matches published Shimmer case studies. The exact socket labels were not in the sources I could read, so verify with Shimmer GSR+ User Guide and the optical pulse probe page.

3. Using it on the page

  1. Use Chrome or Edge (not Safari). Open the robot page.
  2. Scroll down, below the black robot window. You will see the bar "Sensor and monitor (Shimmer)". Click it if it is closed.
  3. Click Connect Shimmer3R (Bluetooth LE). A small window opens: choose the one named Shimmer3R-xxxx and click Pair.
  4. The Connection log is the dark box right under the buttons. It prints each step. If it says FAILED, copy that line and send it to me.
  5. Below the log, the monitor shows heart rate (needs about 5 seconds of a still finger) and skin conductance.
  6. Click Start recording before a routine, Stop after, then Download CSV.

If the page looks the same as before, hard refresh it (Cmd+Shift+R). The Unity build must also be rebuilt/re-published for the new panel to appear; running the Unity editor alone does not show it.
