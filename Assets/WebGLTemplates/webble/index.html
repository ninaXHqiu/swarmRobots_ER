How to put the sensor on the person

Open the picture guide (which part is which, where it goes) in a new tab

  1. Before you start. Clean, dry hands, no hand cream. Use the hand the person uses less (for example the left hand if they write with the right). They sit with that arm resting on the table or on their lap. Allow about 2 minutes for the skin conductance to settle before recording.
  2. 1. The Shimmer unit (the box). Fix it on the wrist of that arm with its wrist strap, snug but not tight, the box on the back of the wrist like a watch. It must not slide when the hand moves. Switch it on (the light blinks).
  3. 2. GSR electrodes (skin conductance). Two Velcro finger straps, each with a metal electrode, on the index and middle finger, around the middle segment (between the two lower knuckles), electrode against the palm side of the finger. The straps hold the electrodes firmly on the skin, snug, no gaps, not cutting off blood flow. The two electrode leads plug into the unit's GSR / electrode jack (the one marked GSR on the Shimmer GSR+ board).
  4. 3. Pulse probe (heart rate). The optical probe goes on the ring finger of the same hand, fingertip pad against the light (or on the earlobe if the hand moves a lot). Snug, not squeezing: if the fingertip goes white or tingles it is too tight. Its lead plugs into the unit's optical pulse jack. Keep that finger still; most heart-rate failures are caused by moving it.
  5. Cables. Lay the leads along the back of the hand and wrist and tape or strap them down so they do not pull on the electrodes or the probe when the hand moves.
  6. Check. Click Connect Shimmer3R. After about 5 seconds of a still finger the monitor shows a heart rate; skin conductance appears as a slowly moving line. If the heart rate stays "--", check the probe position and keep the hand still. If the conductance is flat at zero, check that the electrode straps touch the skin.

Placement follows common practice for Shimmer GSR+ (finger GSR electrodes, fingertip or ear PPG, non-dominant hand). Check jack labels and exact positions against Shimmer's GSR+ user guide for your unit.
